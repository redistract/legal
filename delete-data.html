Delete your Redistract data

Effective 5 November 2026

Redistract (by Shinu Thomas) keeps your blocked apps, schedules, statistics and learning progress only on your phone. The only account-like data on our server is your optional Remote profile.

Delete everything on your phone

Uninstall Redistract, or open Android Settings > Apps > Redistract > Storage and tap Clear storage. We never receive this data, so there is nothing else to delete.

Delete your Remote profile in the app

  1. Open Redistract and tap the Re:DISTRACT name at the top left of the home screen to open App Details.
  2. Tap Your data & deletion.
  3. Tap Delete Remote profile and confirm.

This immediately deletes from our server your display name, anonymous ID, public keys, push token, friend connections, invitations, blocks and all signals you sent or received, and asks Firebase to delete your anonymous Authentication ID. Your friends will see the connection disappear.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app, email redistractapp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Remote data" and the display name you used in Remote. We will delete the matching profile and everything linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email. We may ask a question to make sure we delete the right profile.

What is not deleted

  • Feedback you sent is not linked to you, so we cannot find it on request. It is deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • Anonymous security records (rate limits and request nonces) expire on their own within a day.
