Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

This policy explains what the Redistract app for Android (package com.redistract.app) does with your information. Redistract is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Almost everything it knows about you stays on your phone.

Who is responsible

Redistract is made by Shinu Thomas, an independent developer, who is the data controller for the little information that leaves your device. Contact: redistractapp@gmail.com

The short version

  • Your blocked apps, schedules, limits, focus sessions, statistics and learning progress are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The Accessibility service only notices which app is open. It never reads what is on your screen or what you type, and it sends nothing anywhere.
  • Information leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs the internet: vocabulary updates, Remote (pairing with friends), and sending feedback.
  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising, and we do not build profiles about you.

Information that stays on your phone

Redistract keeps the following in its private app storage. It is excluded from Android backups, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings.

  • The apps you mark as distracting, your schedules, usage limits and settings.
  • Your history of pauses, passes and focus sessions, and the statistics built from them.
  • Your vocabulary and Memory Mix progress, including your answers.
  • Screen-time totals read from Android's Usage Access, used only for your statistics and limits.

Android permissions and why we need them

  • Accessibility service. Detects when an app you chose comes to the front and when you unlock your phone, so Redistract can pause that app. It can also take you to the Home screen or lock the screen while a block you set (or a block a paired friend sent with your permission) is active. It cannot read window content (this is switched off in its configuration), and it never records or sends anything. You turn it on yourself in Android Settings after an explanation in the app, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Display over other apps. Shows the pause screen on top of the app you opened.
  • Usage access. Reads app usage totals on your phone for statistics and daily limits.
  • List of installed apps. Lets you pick which apps count as distracting. The list never leaves your phone.
  • Foreground service and notifications. Keeps protection running reliably and shows a notification while it does, plus focus and reminder notifications.
  • Do Not Disturb access. Only if you turn it on for focus sessions. Redistract restores your previous setting afterwards, and only if it was the one that changed it.
  • Alarms, start at boot and battery settings. Start and end your scheduled focus sessions on time and restore protection after a restart.
  • Camera. Only to scan a friend's pairing QR code. Images are processed on your phone and are never saved or sent.
  • Vibration. Haptic feedback.

Information that leaves your phone

Vocabulary updates

About once a day, and when you download a pack, the app fetches vocabulary files from GitHub Pages (redistract.github.io). Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details to deliver the files. Nothing about you or your learning is sent. GitHub's privacy statement applies to that request.

Remote (optional)

Remote lets you pair with a friend so you can send each other a short app block or a quiz. Nothing is sent until you open Remote and choose a name. When you use it:

  • Google Firebase Authentication gives your installation a random anonymous ID. It processes your IP address to prevent abuse. No email, phone number or Google account is used.
  • Firebase App Check with the Play Integrity API confirms requests come from a genuine copy of Redistract.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone a push token so signals from friends arrive quickly.
  • Our server (run on Cloudflare) stores your anonymous ID, the display name you choose, your app's public keys, your push token, when your app last checked in, whether your phone is ready to receive signals, your friend connections and the permissions you granted, pending invitations (only a keyed hash of the code), people you blocked, and the signals you sent or received with their type, length, timing and status. The content of each signal is end-to-end encrypted between the two phones.

Your paired friends can see your display name, when your app last checked in, whether your phone is ready, and the status of signals between you. They never see your app list, your usage, your screen or your quiz answers.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, the app version and the Android version. To stop spam, the request is checked with Firebase Authentication and App Check, but your anonymous ID is used only for rate limiting and is not stored with your message. We do not receive your name or email address. The message is stored on our server and forwarded to our inbox using Resend, an email delivery service. Because feedback is not linked to you, please do not include personal information in it.

Usage statistics

This version of Redistract does not send usage statistics. If an optional statistics feature is added later, it will be off by default and this policy will be updated before it is released.

Why we use your information (legal bases)

  • To provide Remote and vocabulary updates you ask for (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse, for example App Check and rate limits (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To read and answer feedback you choose to send and improve the app (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf: Google (Firebase), Cloudflare (server and database), Resend (feedback email) and GitHub (vocabulary hosting). Your paired friends see the information described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

International transfers

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data from the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Remote profile, connections and push token: until you delete your Remote profile.
  • Signals: deleted 7 days after they finish. Invitation codes expire after 10 minutes. Security records such as request nonces and rate limits are deleted within days.
  • Feedback: deleted from our server after 180 days. Forwarded emails are deleted when no longer needed.
  • Firebase anonymous ID: deleting your Remote profile in the app signs out and asks Firebase to delete it.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall Redistract, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Redistract > Storage.
  • Remote profile: in the app, open App Details > Your data & deletion > Delete Remote profile. This removes your profile, connections and signals from our server, deletes your push token and asks Firebase to delete your anonymous ID. Your friends will see the connection disappear.
  • Without the app: follow the steps at https://redistract.github.io/legal/delete-data.html or email redistractapp@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your information, and to data portability. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights; we do not sell or share personal information. To use any of these rights, email redistractapp@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days.

Security

Requests to our server use HTTPS, are signed by a key that never leaves your phone, and are verified with App Check. Signal contents are end-to-end encrypted. Local data is protected by Android's app sandbox. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to the minimum.

Children

Redistract is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country (up to 16 in parts of the EU), ask a parent or guardian before using Remote. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will post any change in the app and at https://redistract.github.io/legal/privacy.html and update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already hold, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
